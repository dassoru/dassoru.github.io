ABOUT DASSORU

A visual journal by
Basuru Jayasanka

DASSORU began with a simple instinct: to notice the moments that are easy to pass by. The name represents a way of looking — like a third eye observing the world.

My photography moves through wildlife, nature, landscapes, travel, street life, people and everyday scenes. I am not trying to photograph only the extraordinary. I look for the feeling inside ordinary moments.

The work has taken me through Sri Lanka, India, Myanmar, Seychelles and beyond. DASSORU is both a portfolio and a personal visual journal — a place to keep the photographs that mean something to me and to share the stories behind them.
