WILDLIFE

2000 Vibe

Even this gentleman knows his good side.

One fine morning in Trincomalee, I heard a beautiful call coming from the trees.

I followed the sound and found this magnificent Malabar Grey Hornbill.

I love wild animals, so naturally I stopped to watch.

But there was something about this bird that made me smile.

It stood there proudly, almost as if it knew exactly where the camera was.

The posture. The expression. The confidence.

It felt like watching someone from the 2000s generation posing deliberately for a photograph — as if the bird itself had decided: “Yes, this is my good side.”

For a few seconds, the wild world and the world of human photographs seemed strangely similar.

So I took the photograph.
