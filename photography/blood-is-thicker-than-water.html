Blood Is Thicker Than Water

A father’s little treasure.

They were only playing.

Two little sisters, five and three years old, enjoying an ordinary day near a paddy field in Polgahawela.

They climbed onto a coconut tree, laughed, played and stayed close to each other without thinking about anything else in the world.

I watched them as a father.

What touched me most was not simply their happiness, but the genuine love between them.

There was no pretending. No conditions. No reason needed.

Just two sisters who naturally wanted to be together.

Their world is still small. A coconut tree, a paddy field, a game, a laugh — sometimes that is enough to make a whole day beautiful.

As their father, I know these years will pass quickly. One day they will grow up and these simple moments will become memories.

So I pressed the shutter.

Not just to make a photograph, but to keep a little piece of their childhood.
