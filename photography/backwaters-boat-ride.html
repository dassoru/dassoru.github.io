Backwaters Boat Ride

What if I was in their photograph?

During a boat ride through the backwaters of Alappuzha, I noticed these foreign visitors enjoying the journey just as I was.

They were looking around, experiencing the scenery and, like me, probably trying to preserve a few moments with their cameras.

Then a strange thought came to me.

What if I was in their photograph?

Perhaps somewhere in one of their photographs, I might simply be another person enjoying the backwaters — a small part of their memory of India.

I wondered how I might look through someone else's lens.

So I photographed them.

Not only to remember them, but to imagine myself through someone else's lens.

Photography sometimes allows us to see the world from the other side of the camera.
