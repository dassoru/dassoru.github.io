PEOPLE

Innocent Smile

The beauty of being a child.

They were simply playing near a paddy field in Polgahawela, climbing and sitting on a coconut tree as children naturally do.

There was nothing extraordinary happening. No special place, no planned photograph, no perfect arrangement.

But their smiles stopped me.

At just five and three years old, their smiles carried something that is difficult to find in the world around us — something completely innocent, untouched and unpolluted.

I looked at them and felt their happiness reach somewhere deep inside me.

Sometimes, the most beautiful photographs are not created by beautiful places. They are created by beautiful moments.

And this was one of those moments I wanted to keep forever.
